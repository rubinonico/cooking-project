## Bias correction

The resolver's alias map collapsed 4 pairs; two of these merge a shelf-stable form onto a fresh one. The effect:

- **Full ratio**: aliased gives a WORSE (higher) ratio at 2.609 vs 2.583 — the bias does NOT flatter Want A on total parts count. This is because the largest cuisine (american) uses more of the aliased forms than other cuisines, so its size is discounted more.
- **SS ratio**: aliased gives a BETTER (lower) ratio at 2.286 vs 2.450 — the bias DOES flatter Want A on shelf-stable parts. Merging `garlic_powder → garlic` hides a shelf-stable ingredient (garlic_powder) behind a method_dependent one (garlic), shrinking the SS union and improving the SS ratio. This is the direction the bias ticket warned about.

The conservative headline picks the worst ratio from either version for each metric.
