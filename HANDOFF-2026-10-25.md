## Traps — read before editing anything

- **`larder/corpus.py` self-tests on import.** If a normaliser change breaks it, it raises before printing
  any number. That is deliberate — name normalisation was the **single largest source of wrong numbers in
  this project** (four separate failures, none of which ever raised an error).
- **Do NOT strip preservation words** (`dried`/`powdered`/`canned`). The FORM is the answer to "can I stock
  this". They are extracted as tags. Equally, do NOT merge `garlic powder` onto `garlic` or `coriander seed`
  onto `coriander` — both are pinned as must-NOT-change cases in the self-test.
- **Food.com's `steps` field is polluted with image URLs** in the `Karo8870` mirror. A "32% have steps"
  count is largely image lists. Take the split from TIMES, never from `steps` on this mirror.
- **`Vatazh0k/` and `AkashPS11/recipes_data_food.com` are the same truncated 34 MB file** — 1,048,543 rows
  with only 1,228 populated (0.1%). The most-downloaded mirror is the broken one. Spot-check a middle row
  before trusting any dataset.
- **Set cover is the WRONG objective for the pantry question** — tried three ways, degenerated all three
  times (`salt`; then carrot/parsley/milk/ginger/lime; then carrot/parsley/lime/baking soda). A minimum-cover
  answer is won by whatever overlaps everyone blandly, which is the opposite of a base. Do not re-run it.
- **Any objective rewarding SHARED attributes across a set has a degenerate optimum.** It has now bitten
  four times here (all-Italian week; three near-identical biryanis; 40-ingredient recipe winning; the three
  set covers). Always sweep the missing constraint and publish the trade-off.
- **This box: bash pipes intermittently die with fork errors under load.** Check multi-file facts in Python.
  Write probes to files, not inline command strings.
